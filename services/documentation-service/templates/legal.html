Merchant Agreement Last Updated: June 16, 2026

Seller Merchant Agreement

This agreement governs the relationship between WeMall Inc. and independent merchants ("Sellers") listing products on the WeMall platform.

1. Complete Seller Indemnity

The Seller agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold WeMall harmless against any and all claims, lawsuits, actions, or damages (including product liability, personal injury, property damage, or death) resulting from any product listed, sold, or distributed by the Seller.

2. Intellectual Property (IP) Infringement

  • The Seller represents and warrants that they own or hold the necessary licenses for all product images, brand names, and descriptions listed.
  • If WeMall receives a copyright/trademark infringement complaint (DMCA/IP notice), WeMall reserves the right to immediately take down the listing, freeze the Seller's escrowed funds, and suspend the Seller account without liability.
  • The Seller shall bear all legal costs and damages incurred by WeMall in connection with any IP infringement claim related to the Seller's store listings.

3. Fees, Payment Escrow, and Clawback Rights

  • Escrow Hold: WeMall reserves the right to hold transaction funds in escrow for up to 30 days post-delivery to cover chargebacks, refunds, or customer disputes.
  • Clawback Right: If a customer issues a bank chargeback, or if fraud is suspected, WeMall has the unilateral right to deduct the disputed amount directly from the Seller's wallet balance, pending payouts, or future sales.

4. Unilateral Termination

WeMall reserves the right to suspend or terminate any Seller account at any time, for any reason, with or without notice, and without financial penalty or liability to the Seller.

Delivery Network Last Updated: June 16, 2026

Rider Services Agreement

This agreement outlines the service standard and independent contractor parameters for delivery agents ("Riders") executing deliveries on the WeFast network.

1. Classification Safeguards (Independent Contractor)

  • No Employment Relationship: The Rider is an independent contractor. This Agreement does not create an employer-employee relationship, partnership, agency, or joint venture.
  • Complete Control: The Rider is free to work for other delivery or rideshare platforms (including direct competitors). WeFast does not control the Rider's work schedule, routes, or method of delivery.
  • Labor Dispute Waiver: The Rider waives any right to file class action lawsuits claiming employee misclassification. Any labor dispute must be resolved on an individual arbitration basis.

2. Full Rider Indemnification & Liability

  • Vehicle Liability: The Rider is solely liable for any accidents, injuries, deaths, or property damage caused while executing deliveries.
  • Liability Hold Harmless: The Rider agrees to indemnify and hold WeFast and WeMall completely harmless from any lawsuits or claims resulting from the Rider's actions, traffic violations, negligent driving, or criminal conduct.
  • Insurance: The Rider must maintain primary commercial automobile liability insurance. WeFast's secondary insurance (if any) only applies after the Rider's primary insurance limits are fully exhausted.
